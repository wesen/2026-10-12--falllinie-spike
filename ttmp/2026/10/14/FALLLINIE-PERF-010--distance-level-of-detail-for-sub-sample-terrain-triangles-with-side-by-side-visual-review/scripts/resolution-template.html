How this was made

Frames come from the deterministic replay (seed 42 unless noted, 600 frames unless noted), captured from each window's framebuffer after the measured loop at five checkpoints. The halftone dot pitch scales with the internal width, so dots keep the same size on screen at both sizes.

Timing uses mirrored device runs with the muted audio producer running: the replay at each size, then live autoplay with launcher settings. GPU busy is GPU-active cycles per second over the 800 MHz clock; near 100% means no headroom left.
