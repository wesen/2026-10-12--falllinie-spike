How this was made

Each 16-row terrain block keeps all columns and its two boundary rows, and draws every 2nd, 4th, 8th or 16th row in between as its distance from the camera doubles past the near distance (120 m or 60 m). Blocks share full boundary rows, so they cannot crack apart.

GPU busy is GPU-active cycles per second over the 800 MHz clock; near 100% means no headroom left. Frames come from the deterministic replay (seed 42 unless noted, 600 frames unless noted) captured after the measured loop, at five checkpoints. Timing is the mean of mirrored device runs with the muted audio producer running at 384×288; the big-scene column is 512×384 without audio, where the GPU is overloaded and savings show directly. Geometry cycles are whole-device counter rates divided by the loop rate.

Red marks every pixel whose colour differs in any channel from the baseline capture.
